Help

How do I pause?

Press P or Esc. The game also pauses when you switch tabs.

How do I play on a phone?

On-screen buttons appear under the game on touch devices. Tap Start game, then use the buttons.

Why is my score not on the global board?

You need a name of at least two characters and an internet connection. If the board shows Offline, scores stay on your device.

Can I change my name?

Yes. Edit the Playing as field in the leaderboard section. Past scores keep the name they were posted with.
